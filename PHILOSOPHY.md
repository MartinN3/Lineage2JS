# Project Philosophy — Preservation Over Monetization

Lineage2JS exists to preserve the technical and cultural legacy of classic Lineage II, enable experimentation, and support developers interested in understanding, rebuilding, and extending the original game experience.

We believe that games should reward time, skill, cooperation, and exploration rather than the willingness or ability to spend real-world money.

We strongly oppose:

- Selling equipment, character power, progression, or competitive advantages for real-world money.
- Paid random rewards, loot boxes, and gambling-like monetization.
- Artificial gameplay restrictions designed to pressure players into spending money.
- Monetization practices that undermine fair competition or exploit player psychology.

We support free experimentation, community development, educational use, private servers, and responsible preservation efforts.

## Our position on licensing

We believe that preserving software requires the freedom to study, modify, redistribute, and build upon it. These freedoms remain important even when other developers make choices we disagree with.

This philosophy expresses the values and intentions of this fork. It does not impose additional legal restrictions on the rights granted by applicable open-source licenses, grant rights to third-party game assets, or resolve the licensing questions described in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

We encourage anyone building on Lineage2JS to respect these principles and contribute to a future where classic games remain accessible, fair, and enjoyable.
